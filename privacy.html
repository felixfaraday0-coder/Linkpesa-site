# LINKPESA – Privacy Notice (draft template)

**Draft only.** Complete this notice and obtain appropriate legal advice before collecting personal data.

**Data controller:** [Legal business/person name and contact details]

**Information collected:** Only information needed to administer membership and payouts, such as name, email, phone number, member ID, referral activity, commission status and limited payout references.

**Purposes:** Membership administration, referral attribution, fraud prevention, commission reconciliation, customer support and legally required record keeping.

**Sharing:** Relevant conversion data may be shared with the advertiser whose offer a member promotes. Personal data should not be sold. Explain any service providers used.

**Security and retention:** Restrict spreadsheet access, enable two-step verification, keep backups securely and delete information when no longer needed unless law requires retention.

**Your choices and rights:** Explain how people can request access, correction or deletion, object where applicable, and contact the business about privacy concerns.

**Contact:** [privacy email / phone]

Do not collect passwords, M-Pesa PINs, OTPs or unnecessary identity documents. Publish this notice only after filling in the controller identity, retention periods, recipients and contact details.
